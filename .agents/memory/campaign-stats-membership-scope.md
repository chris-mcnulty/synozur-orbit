---
name: Campaign statistics membership scope
description: Active-membership requirement for outreach campaign conversion and yield statistics.
---
Campaign totals, yield, reply rates, funnels and signal analysis must exclude archived, removed or deleted campaign contacts from both numerators and denominators.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires campaign stats to describe the current contact set, rather than retained historical contacts.

**How to apply:** Retain communication history for auditing, but do not count inactive memberships in campaign performance. Refresh displayed statistics after membership changes.
