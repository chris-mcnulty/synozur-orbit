---
name: Campaign statistics membership scope
description: Active-membership requirement for outreach campaign conversion and yield statistics.
---
Campaign totals, yield, reply rates, funnels and signal analysis must exclude archived, removed or deleted campaign contacts from both numerators and denominators.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires campaign stats to describe the current contact set, rather than retained historical contacts.

**How to apply:** Retain communication history for auditing, but do not count inactive memberships in campaign performance. Refresh displayed statistics after membership changes.

Campaign progress must reflect recorded conversations outside Orbit, not just Orbit sends. Keep actual conversations, internal message sends, and worked/researched contacts distinguishable; shared meetings represent one meeting, not one per person.

**Why:** The user obtains many meetings through conversations outside Orbit and expects campaign progress to represent that work.

**How to apply:** Count attributable logged external activity, expose meetings and their companies, and distinguish agreements awaiting scheduling from booked meetings. Do not infer unseen mailbox activity.

Operators must be able to mark review/preparation reminders done themselves, with an undo path. Completion is not evidence that any outreach was sent or that a restriction was removed.

**Why:** The user explicitly notes that Orbit cannot observe work such as review and meeting preparation.

**How to apply:** Respect manual completion until relevant evidence changes. Never equate hiding a reminder with performing its underlying external action.
