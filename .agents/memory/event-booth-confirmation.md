---
name: Explicit booth confirmation
description: Event promotion must not infer booth participation from general marketing context.
---

Event copy may describe booth participation only when the event has explicitly confirmed booth details. Missing or blank details mean no confirmed booth, not permission to infer one.

**Why:** Events may involve speaking or attendance without exhibiting. General theme or voice-profile language previously caused incorrect “stop by our booth” invitations for non-exhibiting events.

**How to apply:** Preserve this distinction across future event-content generators. Event-specific confirmation takes precedence over generic marketing context; do not assume that conference attendance implies a booth.
