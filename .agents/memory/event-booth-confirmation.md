---
name: Explicit booth confirmation
description: Event promotion must not infer booth participation from general marketing context.
---

Event copy may describe booth participation only when the event has explicitly confirmed booth details. Missing or blank details mean no confirmed booth, not permission to infer one.

**Why:** Events may involve speaking or attendance without exhibiting. General theme or voice-profile language previously caused incorrect “stop by our booth” invitations for non-exhibiting events.

**How to apply:** Preserve this distinction across future event-content generators. Event-specific confirmation takes precedence over generic marketing context; do not assume that conference attendance implies a booth.

Main-event briefs may contain historical sessions and presenters even when the event has no separate schedule. Historical facts are not confirmation of a current session or team attendance. Preserve complete saved factual statements without allowing newly assembled claims; never use history to bypass explicit booth confirmation.

**Why:** A follow-up roundtable's brief described a previous conference presentation. Blanket session/speaker keyword rejection blocked generation despite the background being supplied by the user. Contradictory prompts also encouraged session/team invitations while forbidding them.

**How to apply:** Keep registration-focused main-event instructions consistent with validation, distinguish past context from the current schedule, and record failed rule categories without logging private briefs or generated copy.
