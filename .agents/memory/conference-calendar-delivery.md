---
name: Conference calendar delivery consent
description: External calendar delivery consent and uncertain-response retry policy for booked conference meetings.
---

A manually booked conference meeting or saved scheduling link is not permission to send calendar invitations. Invitation delivery and cancellation each need an explicit review and confirmation. The connected seller's Outlook mailbox is the organizer; the manually named meeting owner is not authority to access another person's calendar.

**Why:** The user explicitly required reviewed external delivery, owner information, safe retries, and cancellation handling without changing the manual booking workflow.

**How to apply:** Keep private prep notes out of attendee-facing agendas. After an uncertain provider response, reconcile the original invitation rather than automatically sending another. Only a definitive rejection permits a new reviewed creation attempt.
