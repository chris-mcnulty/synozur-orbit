---
name: Competitor suggestion relevance
description: CRM deal activity is not a competitor signal; recommendations must be relevant to the active market.
---

Do not recommend tenant-wide CRM companies as competitors based on having deals. Use market-specific competitive context instead.

**Why:** During CSI onboarding, Synozur clients and consulting firms appeared as competitor suggestions because they had CRM deals, not because they competed with CSI. This is misleading even in the default market: customers and prospects are not inherently competitors.

**How to apply:** Any future HubSpot-powered recommendations need evidence of competition and relevance to the selected market. Preserve intentional CRM links and manually selected competitors; do not delete existing records as part of correcting recommendation logic.
