## Global style spec (prepend or set once per clip)

**STYLE:** Cinematic, premium B2B tech. Dark, moody base with volumetric light. Synozur brand accents — electric purple (#810FFB) and magenta (#E60CB3) gradients used as practical and edge lighting. Clean, uncluttered compositions with generous negative space. Shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, soft anamorphic highlights, realistic 24fps. Restrained and confident, never cheesy corporate stock.

**NEGATIVE (avoid):** garbled or readable on-screen text, watermarks, logos, distorted hands or faces, extra fingers, cluttered desks, clip-art, cartoonish, oversaturated neon, low quality.

**PRODUCTION RULES:**
- Do **not** rely on the model to render words — all on-screen text and the Synozur logo are added in post (CapCut / Premiere). Generated text always garbles.
- Generate 2–3 variations per shot; pick the cleanest motion.
- Keep camera moves slow and deliberate to match the wartime, anti-hype tone.

## Shots

### Shot 1 — Hook (0:00–0:06)
- **VO:** "Ninety-five percent of AI pilots never make it out of the demo. That's MIT's number, and it hasn't moved this year."
- **Prompt:** A vast, dark high-tech lab. Dozens of small glowing holographic project cards float in the air; most flicker and fade to cold grey, only a few stay lit with purple light. Slow dolly push-in. Volumetric purple and magenta haze, shallow depth of field, cinematic.
- **Camera:** Slow dolly in. · **On-screen text (post):** large **95%**

### Shot 2 — Reframe (0:06–0:12)
- **VO:** "Here's the part nobody wants to hear. That's not an AI problem. It's a discipline problem."
- **Prompt:** Macro close-up of a single glowing purple holographic project card hovering above an open human palm in a dark space; the card dims as the hand slowly closes. Shallow depth of field, soft rim light, film grain.
- **Camera:** Static with subtle handheld drift. · **On-screen text:** none

### Shot 3 — Pilot purgatory (0:12–0:22)
- **VO:** "I've watched companies run five pilots in a year and still not point to a single dollar on the P&L. The demos looked great. Then they just sat there."
- **Prompt:** Five identical glowing prototype cubes on dark pedestals in a dim museum-like gallery, fine dust settling on them, their light fading one by one. Slow lateral tracking dolly past the row. Moody purple key light, deep shadows, cinematic.
- **Camera:** Slow lateral dolly (left to right). · **On-screen text:** none

### Shot 4 — The 5% (0:22–0:27)
- **VO:** "The five percent that actually scale do three unglamorous things."
- **Prompt:** A single bright purple line of light ignites and races forward across a dark reflective floor, splitting into three glowing nodes in the distance. Low camera angle close to the floor, forward dolly. High contrast, magenta edge glow.
- **Camera:** Forward dolly, low angle. · **On-screen text (post):** **1 · 2 · 3**

### Shot 5 — One: backlog (0:27–0:37)
- **VO:** "One. They build a use-case backlog from real work. Not the flashy demo. The actual documents and workflows, ranked by the money they move."
- **Prompt:** Top-down view of hands sorting and stacking glowing translucent document tiles on a dark glass table, arranging them into a single ordered column; the top tile glows brightest magenta. Slow push-in from above, shallow depth of field, premium lighting.
- **Camera:** Top-down, slow push-in. · **On-screen text (post):** **One — backlog**

### Shot 6 — Two: governance (0:37–0:48)
- **VO:** "Two. They set governance before they scale, not after the audit. Who owns the output. What data it touches. How you'd defend it tomorrow."
- **Prompt:** A translucent purple shield-dome forms over a glowing data core in a dark, architectural control room, sealing into place just before the core scales up in size. Slow orbit around the core. Clean, geometric, volumetric light.
- **Camera:** Slow orbit. · **On-screen text (post):** **Two — governance**

### Shot 7 — Three: human owner (0:48–0:58)
- **VO:** "Three. They put a human owner on every AI output. No exceptions. AI speeds up the work. It does not replace the judgment."
- **Prompt:** A person's silhouette at a dark desk rests a hand on a glowing AI output panel; a single clean purple signature line draws itself beneath the panel. Cinematic rim light, medium close-up, shallow depth of field.
- **Camera:** Slow push to the hand. · **On-screen text (post):** **Three — human owner**

### Shot 8 — Transformation vs theater (0:58–1:04)
- **VO:** "None of that is exciting. All of it is what separates transformation from theater."
- **Prompt:** Split composition: on the left a chaotic flickering stage crossed by harsh spotlights and smoke; on the right a calm, steadily lit purple operational corridor receding into depth. Slow reveal sliding from the chaotic side to the calm side. Cinematic contrast.
- **Camera:** Slow lateral reveal (left to right). · **On-screen text:** none

### Shot 9 — The real fix (1:04–1:10)
- **VO:** "If your pilots are stuck, the fix is almost never a better model. It's a better operating discipline."
- **Prompt:** Macro shot of a precise machine of interlocking purple-lit gears turning smoothly and quietly in the dark, perfectly meshed, satisfying mechanical motion. Tight depth of field, soft magenta highlights, film grain.
- **Camera:** Macro, locked off with gear motion. · **On-screen text:** none

### Shot 10 — CTA / end card (1:10–1:15)
- **VO:** "I wrote the whole playbook. Link in the description."
- **Prompt:** A dark frame; a single glowing purple-to-magenta gradient orb drifts to one side and resolves into clean negative space, holding steady. Minimal, premium, calm.
- **Camera:** Static hold. · **On-screen text / brand (post):** Synozur logo (`brand/logos/SA-Logo-Horizontal-white.png`) + **"Pick the fight. Govern it. Ship it."** + CTA.

## Post-production notes
- **VO:** Record in Chris's voice (preferred) or a calm, confident AI voice; pace ~2.6 words/sec to fit ~70 sec.
- **Music:** Minimal, tense-to-resolve underscore; low bed, lifts slightly at the three disciplines, resolves on the end card.
- **Text & logo:** All overlays and the logo added in edit, not generated. Use Synozur purple (#810FFB) / magenta (#E60CB3) for lower-thirds.
- **Pacing:** Hard cut on each VO beat; keep clips tight (trim AI clips to the strongest 4–6 seconds).
- **Captions:** Burn in open captions (most LinkedIn/Shorts views are muted).
