# Polaris Podcast Outline — Standard Format

Polaris is Synozur's podcast. Episodes are **recorded live**, so the deliverable is a
written outline (branded Word doc), not AI-generated audio. This documents the standard
structure so generated outlines match Synozur's house format.

Reference example: `docs/polaris-outline-example.docx` (Petar Kralev / Mirror 360 episode).

## Fixed facts (reuse every episode)

- **Host:** Chris McNulty, Chief Technology Officer, Synozur.
- **Show name:** Polaris (sometimes "Polaris Pathways" in the spoken intro).
- **Show page:** polaris.synozur.com · **Email:** polaris@synozur.com
- **Events page:** synozur.com/events
- **Synozur boilerplate:** "Synozur — the transformation company — reimagines business for our clients, navigating strategies and leadership culture with ease."
- **Production credits (outro):** produced with help from Riverside.fm; theme song "Alternative Dream" courtesy of Adobe; additional music/sound by Indie Guy Records; graphic design by Josh Brantley.
- **Sign-off:** "I'm Chris McNulty, and this has been Polaris, a show that charts the course for business and technology transformation from Synozur. Learn more at synozur.com. See you next time. Keep following your North Star."

## Header block

- Title: "Polaris Podcast Outline"
- Guest: name, title, company
- Episode Title
- Prep line: "Prepared {date}  ·  Draft for guest review"

## Section order

1. **Cold Open** — scripted host hook monologue. Opens "From Synozur, this is Polaris. I'm Chris McNulty." Sets up the episode's central tension, ends "This is Polaris." May include a `[GRAPHIC: ...]` production cue.
2. **Intro** — four beats:
   - Chris welcomes audience ("Welcome to Polaris Pathways. I'm your host, Chris McNulty, Chief Technology Officer at Synozur.")
   - Explain Synozur (boilerplate above)
   - Introduce guest (short bio paragraph)
   - Transition: "He'll join us in a few minutes, but first…"
3. **Trends** — define terms and data points with cited stats; note that the reading list / references go in the show notes. Ends with a handoff to bring the guest in.
4. **Topic of the Day — {episode theme}**
   - **Introduction** — welcome the guest on air.
   - Warm-up: "Question: Where are you calling from today?" + "Suggested Answer:".
   - **Interview** — a series of **Question / Suggested Answer** pairs. Questions are prepped, conversational, and reference the guest's story; Suggested Answers are short prompts the guest can review before recording.
   - **Personal Impact** — Question asking for a recent book/film/song/etc. with personal meaning (outside business/tech) + Suggested Answer.
   - **Staying in Touch** — Question on how the audience can follow the guest + Suggested Answer.
   - **Closing** — host recaps guest + latest news; guest thanks.
5. Transition line: "We'll be right back…"
6. **Events** — 2–4 upcoming events with dates/locations; points to show notes, the Synozur blog, and synozur.com/events; invites event submissions.
7. **Coming Soon** — teaser for the next episode/guest. Include a `[Confirm next-episode teaser before final recording.]` cue.
8. **Outro** — wrap, thank guest, show page (polaris.synozur.com), feedback email (polaris@synozur.com), subscribe/rate/review ask, production credits, and the standard sign-off.

## Conventions

- Interview content is written as **"Question:" / "Suggested Answer:"** pairs — it's a guest-review draft, not a verbatim script.
- Bracketed cues `[GRAPHIC: ...]`, `[Confirm ...]`, and parenthetical "(Note: ...)" mark production/editorial to-dos.
- Tone: warm, intelligent, plain-spoken; light personal/cultural touches.
